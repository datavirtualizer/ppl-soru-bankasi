# ICAO Annex 1–19 · Görüntü Kartı Promptları

Grok Imagine (grok.com veya X uygulaması), Gemini, ChatGPT — hepsinde çalışır.
Prompt'lar İngilizce; görüntü modelleri İngilizce ile belirgin şekilde daha iyi sonuç verir.

**Kullanım:** Aşağıdaki ORTAK STİL satırını her prompt'un sonuna ekle. Kartların
birbirine benzemesi önemli — beyin, aynı görsel dile sahip bir seti tek blok olarak
hatırlıyor.

## Ortak stil (her prompta ekle)

```
Flat vector illustration, bold clean outlines, single centered subject, solid pastel
background, aviation flashcard style, high contrast, square 1:1, no words or letters
anywhere in the image.
```

## Kartlar

**1 — Personel Lisanslandırma**
```
A giant numeral 1 standing tall in the centre, clearly readable, its vertical stroke
formed by a pilot in uniform standing at attention and holding up a license card.
```

**2 — Hava Kuralları**
```
A giant numeral 2 in the centre, clearly readable, drawn as a two-way airway: a road-like
corridor with dashed centre lane markings curving into the shape of a 2, one lane with an
airliner heading away and the opposite lane with an airliner coming back, small round
blue rule signs standing along the edges, sky background.
```

**3 — Meteorolojik Hizmetler**
```
A fluffy white cumulus cloud floating horizontally, its puffy upper edge formed by the
numeral 3 lying flat on its back and rotated ninety degrees, the three rounded humps of
the 3 clearly readable as the bumps of the cloud, rain falling from the left end and
warm sunshine breaking through the right end.
```

**4 — Havacılık Haritaları**
```
The numeral 4 constructed out of a compass rose, lying on top of an unfolded
aeronautical chart with route lines and waypoints.
```

**5 — Ölçü Birimleri**
```
A giant numeral 5 in the centre, correctly oriented and not mirrored, with its straight
flat bar across the top, its stem going down on the left, and its round belly bulging to
the right. The whole 5 is built from a yellow folding carpenter ruler, and an open hand
with five fingers spread wide rests beside it measuring a span, aviation gauges behind.
```

**6 — Hava Aracının İşletilmesi (OPS)**
```
A giant numeral 6 in the centre, clearly readable, its round bowl formed by a spinning
landing gear wheel and its stem by the gear strut, under an aircraft belly touching down
on a runway with motion blur.
```

**7 — Tescil İşaretleri**
```
The numeral 7 shaped exactly like an aircraft vertical tail fin, with a registration
marking painted across it, rest of the plane fading out.
```

**8 — Uçuşa Elverişlilik**
```
The numeral 8 tipped onto its side to become an infinity symbol, a small airplane
flying an endless loop through it, certificate stamp glow.
```

**9 — Kolaylaştırma**
```
The numeral 9 whose long tail turns into a winding queue of passengers with suitcases
at a passport control desk.
```

**10 — Havacılık Haberleşmesi**
```
A vintage telephone handset merged with a pilot headset, the numeral 10 as its rotary
dial, radio waves rippling outward.
```

**11 — Hava Trafik Hizmetleri (ATS)**
```
A giant numeral 11 in the centre, clearly readable, its two vertical strokes formed by
two thick bright vapour trails drawn by two airliners flying side by side, a dashed
measuring line marking the separation gap between the two strokes, glowing green radar
sweep below.
```

**12 — Arama ve Kurtarma (SAR)**
```
The numeral 12 glowing on the searchlight beam of a rescue helicopter hovering over
dark sea waves at night, life raft below.
```

**13 — Kaza ve Olay İncelemesi**
```
The numeral 13 cracked and split down the middle, an orange black-box flight recorder
lying in front of it, caution tape.
```

**14 — Havaalanları**
```
The numeral 14 painted large as a runway threshold marking, control tower and
approach lights in the background.
```

**15 — Havacılık Bilgi Hizmetleri (AIS)**
```
The numeral 15 displayed on a split-flap airport information board, NOTAM-style
screen glowing in a terminal.
```

**16 — Çevresel Koruma**
```
The numeral 16 formed from two oxygen atoms bonded as an O2 molecule, green leaves
sprouting around a jet engine with clean air flowing through it.
```

**17 — Havacılık Güvenliği**
```
A giant numeral 17 dominating the centre, clearly readable: the 1 is a tall metal
detector arch and the 7 is an X-ray scanner belt bent into a seven shape beside it,
uniformed security officer silhouette standing between them.
```

**18 — Tehlikeli Maddeler**
```
The numeral 18 stamped inside a red warning triangle on a sealed cargo crate,
hazard diamond symbols, cargo hold lighting.
```

**19 — Emniyet Yönetimi (SMS)**
```
The numeral 19 at the top of a glowing control-room dashboard covered in safety
gauges and risk dials, a supervisor silhouette overseeing everything.
```

## Notlar

- "no words or letters" demene rağmen model bazen yazı sıkıştırır; Türkçe metin
  istemek daha da kötü sonuç verir. Kart üstündeki başlığı sonradan kendin ekle.
- Rakamın görünmesi şart — çengelin tamamı rakamın şeklinde. Rakam çıkmadıysa
  prompt'a "the numeral X must be clearly visible and form the main shape" ekle.
- 15 ve 16'nın çengelleri diğerlerinden zayıf; görüntü onları taşıyacak, o yüzden
  bu ikisini beğenmezsen tekrar üret.
